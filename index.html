Better Loot Tables

26.2

With the goal of making exploration more worthwhile and rewarding, Better Loot Tables modifies virtually every naturally-generated chest in the vanilla game to include stronger gear, relevant materials, and more interesting loot in general.

My first foray into datapack creation, this has become a staple in basically all of my singleplayer and multiplayer worlds.

  • Recently Updated

Better Campfires

26.3

Designed for the early game, Better Campfires adds additional cooking recipes to the campfire to increase its functionality. With the pack installed, use the campfire to craft torches, dry rotten flesh into leather, heat up clay, and more.

Better Dyes

26.3

Inspired by Hermitcraft and Vanilla Tweaks, Better Dyes is an attempt to unify how blocks are dyed and redyed in the base game. Through new recipes, all dyable blocks (except for glazed terracotta) can be dyed using the 2x2 and 3x3 crafting grids using any combination of already-dyed blocks.

  • Discontinued

Leatherworking

1.21.10

An old experiment datapack where you could un-craft leather armor back into items, losing a piece or two in the process. I have not maintained or updated this datapack since its initial creation, and it is no longer available for download.
